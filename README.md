# Country Time Spades ♠️

> Spades the way it's played at the cookout — voice-driven avatars, real trash talk, and the table energy of four people face-to-face. Southern Black card-culture, preserved and playable.

**Status:** Research & Ideation (Phase 0)

---

## The Vision

Most Spades apps feel like a card simulator: clean, quiet, and *sterile*. They strip out the thing that makes Spades a living cultural institution — the talk. The leaning over the table. The "I ain't sayin what I got, but you gon' find out." The way a hand ends in laughter or a pointed finger, not a score popup.

Country Time Spades is built on one conviction: **the avatar across from you should feel like a person sitting at the table.** Characters speak to you (optional text-first fallback), react to big plays, remember the score, and keep the game feeling like a Friday night card game, not a solitaire skin.

Inspired by *Ready 2 Rumble Boxing* (the excitable, announcer-driven energy) and informed by the best of the Apple Arcade *Spades* (polish and accessibility) — but pivoted hard toward personality, voice, and cultural authenticity.

## Core Differentiators

1. **Talking avatars** — voice-first table banter with a full text-first mode that *feels* the same
2. **Cookout authenticity** — JJDD (Joker-Joker-Deuce-Deuce), trash talk, "board" / "set" / "in the hole" vernacular dialed in
3. **Reactive characters** — avatars react to reneges, big hands, nils, and near-misses like real tablemates
4. **Announcer energy** — "Let's get ready to deal" style hype moments, music stingers, callouts
5. **Accessibility first** — one-tap speak, or type; never required to use the mic
